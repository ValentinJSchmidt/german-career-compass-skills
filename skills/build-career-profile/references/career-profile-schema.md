# Career Profile Schema

Use this schema for in-session matching only. Never write a filled profile into shared
skill resources.

## Canonical Six Dimensions

`career-compass`, `find-career-options`, and both `discover-*` skills gate on these six
being present before they will search. Use identical names everywhere:

1. **Situation** — where the person is right now: employment status, current role as
   *tasks*, urgency, income floor, energy and health as it affects work, notice period
2. **Skills** — what they can actually do, with evidence, from any source: paid work, care
   work, self-employment, military, volunteering, serious hobbies — plus formal
   qualifications, licences, and foreign-credential recognition status
3. **Motivation** — what they want more and less of; what a good workday looks like; what
   they would miss from the current job
4. **Constraints** — location and radius, remote/hybrid/onsite, hours, childcare windows,
   shift tolerance, mobility, physical limits, language, work permit, ties to people and
   place (see `constraints-checklist.md`)
5. **Direction** — appetite for change (grow where I am / sideways / change direction), time
   and money available for training, risk tolerance
6. **No-gos** — hard exclusions: shift patterns, environments, tasks, industries, travel,
   customer contact, relocation

Jobs, certificates, and courses are not separate dimensions — they are the evidence used to
fill Skills, Motivation, and No-gos.

Mark every constraint as **hard** or **flexible**. Discovery treats hard constraints as
filters and may only relax them through the ladder in
`find-career-options/references/self-correction-ladder.md`, out loud, with consent.

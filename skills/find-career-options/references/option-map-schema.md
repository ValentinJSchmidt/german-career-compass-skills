# Option Map Schema

The person-facing output. Group by what the person cares about — the kind of work, or the
track — never by source axis, employer size, or search order.

## Required map-level notes

Open with the **visibility caveat**, always, in the person's language:

> Not every job is on the internet. In trades, care, and small firms, a lot of hiring
> happens through a notice in the window, a call, or someone who knows someone — those
> employers often never post anything. So a short list here means "little was findable
> online", not "there is little work". Where I've marked an option *usually not advertised*,
> the way in is a phone call or a visit, and I've put the contact details there.

Close with a **search coverage note**: which sources were used, which were thin or
unavailable, what share came from job aggregators, how many options are `offline_likely`,
and the date. Keep it three or four lines.

If the ladder in `self-correction-ladder.md` was walked, show that too: which rungs, what
each produced, and which constraint is binding.

## Per option

- **What it is** — the occupation or role, in plain words
- **Where** — employer or area, distance from home, and whether it works with the person's
  actual transport
- **Why it fits you** — grounded in the profile: which of their skills carry over, which
  part of what they wanted this delivers. Specific, not flattering.
- **What the work is actually like** — the day in tasks, not the job title
- **How you'd get in** — entry route, realistic duration, cost, and what employers around
  here actually ask for as opposed to the formal requirement
- **AI outlook** — from `assess-ai-exposure`: which tasks are moving, which are durable,
  what to build. Task level, dated. Never "safe" or "doomed".
- **Fits your constraints?** — commute, hours, care windows, shifts, physical demands,
  income floor. **Name what does not fit.** A "mostly fits" that hides a night-shift
  requirement is a failure.
- **Visibility** — `posted_online`, `employer page only`, or `usually not advertised`, in
  plain words with what that means for how to approach it
- **How to make contact** — a URL, or a phone number and an address, or the chamber or
  Innung that holds the list. Every option gets a usable next action.
- **Evidence** — source URL and date. Mark postings over eight weeks old as possibly filled.
- **What could make this wrong** — the two or three things to check before committing

## Excluded options

Keep a short list of what was filtered out and why — "requires nights", "42 km, no bus
before 06:00", "needs a licence you don't have". This is useful, not noise: it shows the
search was real, and it lets the person overrule a constraint you were told was hard.

## Tone

Follow `../../career-compass/references/advising-stance.md`. In particular: no option is a
consolation prize, no option is described as beneath the person, and nothing is padded to
make the list look longer.

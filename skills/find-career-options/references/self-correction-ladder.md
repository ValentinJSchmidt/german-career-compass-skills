# Self-Correction Ladder

What to do when the option map comes back thin. Trigger: **fewer than four options survive
hard-constraint filtering.**

## Before climbing: which kind of thin is this?

Two very different problems produce the same empty screen, and they need opposite responses.

**Thin because constrained** — the work exists nearby, but the radius, the hours, the care
window, or the licence genuinely excludes it. The answer is a conversation about which
constraint has any give.

**Thin because invisible** — the work exists nearby and the person could do it, but it is
not posted online. Typical for trades, care, cleaning, hospitality, and firms under about
twenty people. The answer is not to relax anything. It is offline outreach: the chamber
list, the Innung directory, the phone call.

Diagnose before you climb. Signals of *invisible* rather than *constrained*:

- the occupation is a trade, care, or small-firm role
- `discover-local-openings` verified real employers in the radius but found no postings
- the radius contains villages and small towns rather than one city
- the aggregator axis returned almost everything the search found

If it is invisible, **stop here.** Do not widen anything. Report verified employers with
`offline_likely`, hand over the contact paths, and say clearly that this kind of job is
usually filled by a call rather than a portal.

## The ladder

Only for *thin because constrained*. One rung at a time. **Say each rung out loud before
applying it** and show what it produced. Stop as soon as four solid options exist.

1. **Widen the radius one step** — and only within what the profile permits. If the person
   said 30 km is the limit because of the school pickup, 45 km is not a rung, it is a
   violation.
2. **Relax the role title** — adjacent roles with an overlapping task profile. Titles are
   the weakest constraint in the profile and usually the first thing worth loosening.
3. **Relax the sector** — the same tasks in a different industry. Warehouse work exists in
   food, pharma, retail, and machinery, with very different conditions and pay.
4. **Relax the entry requirement** — Quereinstieg, Teilqualifikation, verkürzte Ausbildung,
   or entry roles that train on the job. What employers actually accept is often below the
   formal requirement.
5. **Relax the employment form** — part-time, temp-to-perm, fixed-term, or entry through a
   smaller role at the right employer.
6. **Report honestly.** If it is still thin, name **which constraint is binding and by how
   much**: "Inside 30 km with no evening shifts I found two options. The evening-shift limit
   is what's binding — dropping it would add roughly six more. The radius costs you much
   less." Then let the person choose. Do not choose for them.

## Rules

- **Never silently drop a constraint.** A recommendation that quietly ignores the childcare
  window is worse than no recommendation, because the person only finds out after applying.
- Never relax the income floor. It is not a rung on this ladder at any point.
- Never relax a physical limit, a work-permit constraint, or a licence requirement. These
  are not preferences.
- Health-related limits are not negotiable and are never a rung.
- Walking the ladder is **visible work**, not silent internal search. The person should be
  able to see what was tried and what it cost.
- Do not pad. Four honest options beat nine where five are unreachable.
- If nothing survives even the full ladder, say so, name the binding constraint, and offer
  the two real alternatives: change something structural (a constraint, over time), or use
  the offline routes for work that was never posted.

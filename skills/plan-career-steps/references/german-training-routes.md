# German Training Routes

Route families, shortest-first. **Verify names, durations, costs, and current rules live** —
they change, they differ by chamber and Bundesland, and provider marketing is not evidence.

## Routes that keep you earning

**Internal progression** — more responsibility with the current employer. The cheapest and
most-forgotten route. Costs nothing, risks nothing, and often unlocks the Weiterbildung the
employer will pay for. Always check this before anything else.

**Weiterbildung / Anpassungsfortbildung** — short courses updating skills in the current
occupation. Days to months, often evenings or blocks, frequently employer-funded.

**Teilqualifikation (TQ)** — a full occupation split into modules, each with a certificate
and each making the person employable at that level. **The key route for a short runway or a
hard income floor**: you can work between modules instead of waiting years for a single
qualification. Not available for every occupation — check.

**Aufstiegsfortbildung** — Meister, Fachwirt, Techniker, Betriebswirt. Builds on an existing
qualification, available part-time or in blocks alongside work. Usually 1-3 years part-time.
The main upward route inside trades and commercial work, and often the highest-return one.

**Quereinstieg** — direct entry, learning on the job, with formal qualification later or
never. Common in care, logistics, driving, sales, security, and parts of IT. What employers
actually accept is frequently below the formal requirement — that gap is where this route
lives, and it is found from job postings and phone calls, not from occupation databases.

## Routes that need a runway

**Verkürzte Ausbildung** — a full Ausbildung shortened for prior experience or an earlier
qualification. Often 2 years instead of 3-3.5. Ask the chamber what reduction applies; this
is decided case by case and people routinely do not know they qualify.

**Umschulung** — retraining into a new occupation, typically around 2 years, sometimes
shorter. May be full-time at a provider or in-company. Frequently the route that funding
programmes are built around.

**Ausbildung** — the full dual route, 2-3.5 years, apprentice pay throughout. Realistic at
any age, and adult apprentices are increasingly common, but the pay is the constraint —
check it against the income floor before recommending it.

**Studium** — only where genuinely required. Check whether the occupation actually needs it
or whether a Fachwirt, Techniker, or Meister route reaches the same work faster.

## Recognition of foreign qualifications

If the person holds a qualification from outside Germany, recognition (**Anerkennung**) is
often the highest-value single step available — it can convert years of existing training
into a usable German qualification, and it is routinely not pursued.

Status matters: `anerkannt`, `teilweise anerkannt` (a partial recognition usually names the
exact gap to close, which is far shorter than starting over), `nicht beantragt`. Point to the
official recognition advice services and the responsible body for that occupation. Do not
predict an outcome — the assessment is individual.

## Regulated occupations

Some work legally requires a specific qualification, licence, or register entry: care,
electrical trades, food handling, security, driving, many health occupations, and the
Handwerk trades requiring a Meister for independent practice. Where a rule like this applies,
**find and cite it** rather than assuming — the details differ by trade and by whether the
person is employed or self-employed.

## Choosing between routes

Ask, in this order:

1. Can this be reached without leaving paid work? If yes, that route wins almost always.
2. Is there a modular version that pays along the way?
3. What do employers **in this radius** actually ask for, as opposed to the formal rule?
4. Does the schedule survive the person's fixed care windows and transport?
5. Only then: what is the most complete qualification?

The chamber (IHK for commercial and industrial, HWK for trades) gives free advice on all of
this and will say what shortening applies to a specific person. **That call is usually the
single highest-value first step in any plan**, and it costs nothing.

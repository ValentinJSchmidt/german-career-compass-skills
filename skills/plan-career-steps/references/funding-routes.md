# Funding Routes

## The rule that governs this whole file

**Never tell someone they qualify for anything.** Eligibility depends on individual
circumstances, current law, the responsible office, and often on a caseworker's decision.
Getting it wrong costs the person real money and real months.

Name the route, name the office that decides, name what to bring, and frame it as a question
to ask. "Ask the Agentur whether a Bildungsgutschein is possible for this Umschulung" is
correct. "You'll get a Bildungsgutschein" is not, and is never acceptable.

**Verify every programme live before naming it.** Programme names, conditions, and existence
change: some are discontinued, some are folded into other programmes, some are
Bundesland-specific. Do not carry any of this from memory — check the responsible body's
current page and cite it with a date.

## A concrete starting point

**mein NOW** (`mein-now.de`) — the Bundesagentur's training portal, carried jointly with all
16 Länder and the federal ministry — includes a **Fördernavigator** covering funding options
such as education vouchers and educational leave, alongside the course search and a list of
local advice centres. It replaced KURSNET, which was shut down at the start of 2025.

Verified 2026-09-07. It is a place to look things up and to find the local advice centre —
**not** a source of eligibility rulings. The rule below still governs everything.

## Route families to check

Described as families rather than as current facts. Confirm the present name and status in
the run.

**For people who are unemployed or at risk of unemployment**
Funding for retraining and further training, usually administered through the Agentur für
Arbeit (or the Jobcenter, depending on which benefit system applies). Typically tied to an
advisor's assessment and to certified providers and courses. The decisive conversation is
with the advisor, and it happens **before** enrolling anywhere.

**For people currently employed**
Support for further training while in work, generally routed through the employer and the
Agentur, aimed at occupations undergoing structural change. Conditions depend on firm size
and the type of training. Worth checking even when a person assumes it is not for them.

**For Aufstiegsfortbildung (Meister, Fachwirt, Techniker)**
A dedicated federal support instrument exists for advancement qualifications, distinct from
the unemployment-side instruments, and it is open to people in work. Frequently overlooked.

**For apprentices and people in Ausbildung**
Support toward living costs during a first Ausbildung, subject to age, household, and
distance conditions.

**Bundesland and regional programmes**
Individual Länder run their own further-training subsidies, often with different conditions
and lower thresholds than the federal instruments. Search for the current programme in the
person's Land specifically — this is where a lot of accessible money sits unclaimed.

**Employer-funded**
The most underused route. Many employers pay for further training, especially where it fills
a role they are struggling to fill. For the "grow where I am" track, this is usually the
first thing to try, and `draft-career-outreach` can write the conversation.

**Household income support**
Where a plan reduces income, general household supports may be relevant. These are decided
by their own authorities on their own rules. Mention them only as something to ask about,
never as part of the arithmetic of the plan.

## Before you name any of this

- Confirm the programme currently exists, under that name, with a dated official source.
- Name the deciding body: Agentur für Arbeit, Jobcenter, the chamber, the Land's programme
  office, or the employer.
- Say what to bring: profile, chosen occupation, the concrete course, and the provider.
- Say what has to be true first. Many instruments require the decision **before** the course
  starts — enrolling first can forfeit the funding entirely. This single fact is worth more
  to most people than the list of programmes.

## Never

- State eligibility, an amount, or a duration as fact.
- Advise resigning to become eligible. Voluntary resignation can affect entitlements, and the
  rules are not something to reason about from memory. Tell the person to speak to the
  Agentur **before** giving notice.
- Recommend a specific paid provider. Point to the certified-provider search and the chamber.
- Treat a provider's own marketing as evidence that a course is funded.
